## I. Editorial decisions after the three-lens review (3 Sep 2026)

The built site was reviewed by three independent agents (native Thai copy, fact-check against this register, design critique); 75 findings, all applied except the four below, which are decisions rather than omissions.

1. **Closing time 21:00 is printed.** The register held `hours_closing` as a conflict. On re-reading the evidence it is not a same-level conflict: the venue's own captions state 11.00–21.00 in two reels and on its pavement A-board (level 1), Google Business Profile agrees (level 2), and two reviewers agree; only Wongnai (level 4) and the LINE MAN *delivery* storefront show 20:30. The site prints the venue's own hours, prints 20:30 only as "LINE MAN delivery orders until 20:30", and adds a call-ahead line. Confirmation is still on the launch checklist.
2. **Fourteen printed-menu dish names are listed without prices.** They are read from the venue's own printed menu book and photo board (owner-authored artefacts) via reviewer photographs in `_reference/`. The artefact is the venue's; the photographs are not published. None carries a price, a description or a "signature" label (the reviewer-sourced label on the lobster spaghetti was removed).

### J.2 The booking module on `book.html`

**Venue classification: no reservation platform, staffed channel.** Verified in the first
research pass: eatigo, Chope, TableCheck, HungryHub and Quandoo return no listing, and the
venue's own captions say `สำรองที่นั่ง 📲Line 📞Call 📨Inbox`. So the site composes the
request and hands it to LINE, phone or Messenger. It never claims a table.

- The submit verb is **request**, in both languages. No state anywhere says booked,
  confirmed or reserved — probed as an assertion.
- The time buttons are the venue's **published opening window** (11:00 to 20:30, generated
  from `content/site.json → hours`), labelled on the page as opening hours and not as a
  live table plan. Nothing is drawn as an available or unavailable slot.
- Fields are the five a human needs to answer: date, time, party size, name, one phone.
- **Where the data goes (changed 4 Sep 2026).** The request is posted to an Apps Script Web
  App the restaurant deploys on its own Google account, which appends a row to its own
  booking sheet. Nothing passes through the studio or a third party. The endpoint is blank
  until the restaurant deploys it, and while it is blank the send button does not render and
  the form keeps its LINE hand-off.
- The visible notice lists every field that is sent, names the recipient, states the purpose,
  gives the erasure route (the restaurant's phone) and states retention (the sheet is cleared
  once a year) — PDPA-shaped, in both languages, and placed above the send control. It is
  paired with the payload in `js/ll.js`: **change one and you must change the other.**
- The draft lives in `sessionStorage` so a language switch does not wipe it, and the page
  says so in visible copy.
